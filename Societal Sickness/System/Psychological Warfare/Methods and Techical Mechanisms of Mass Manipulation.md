These are concise lists of methods and technical mechanisms used by UK authorities across surveillance, disinformation, defamation, manipulation, and psychological operations.

Key highlights include GCHQ's bulk collection programs (Tempora), JTRIG's "dirty tricks" (false flags, honey traps, social media manipulation), 77th Brigade's information warfare, and various technical tools like DDoS attacks, virus deployment, and poll manipulation.

# UK Authorities Psychological Operations: Methods and Technical Mechanisms

## Surveillance

- Bulk data collection (Tempora, Mastering the Internet, Global Telecoms Exploitation)
- Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)
- Satellite imagery analysis and processing
- Telecommunications Act 1984 bulk phone data collection
- ECHELON surveillance system
- Special Collection Service / STATEROOM programme
- Internet communications interception via telecom providers
- Close access technical operations (physical observation, hotel computer tapping, phone monitoring)
- Five Eyes collaboration
- Composite Signals Organisation (CSO) gathering

## Disinformation

- Online Covert Action (mass messaging, story pushing)
- False flag operations
- Planting misinformation on blogs and social media
- Social media manipulation (Twitter, Facebook, YouTube, Flickr)
- URL shortening service (Lurl.me) for manipulation and intelligence collection
- Fabricated analyses and false documents
- Honeypot espionage ploys
- Spread of pro-revolution messages during Arab Spring
- Monitoring social media platforms
- Embedding government censors within social media companies

## Defamation

- Discrediting targets
- Emailing and texting colleagues/neighbors with unsavory information
- Changing photographs on social media sites
- Credential harvesting (using journalists to spread information)
- Honey traps (sexual and non-sexual)
- Reputation destruction campaigns
- Targeted smear operations
- Planting negative information about individuals/companies

## Manipulation

- Cognitive hacking
- Manipulating online poll results (UNDERPASS)
- Artificially inflating page view counts (SLIPSTREAM)
- Amplifying sanctioned messages on YouTube (GESTATOR)
- Censoring video content (SILVERLORD)
- Spoofing email addresses and identities (CHANGELING)
- Finding private photographs on Facebook (SPRING BISHOP)
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks
- Computer viruses (Ambassadors Reception: encrypts files, deletes emails, shakes screen, blocks login)
- Controlling narrative through social media
- Shaping the battlefield through information operations

## Psychological Operations (PSYOP)

- Non-lethal psychological warfare
- Information operations
- Media operations (video editing, podcast recording, viral post writing)
- Social media advertising
- Data analytics
- Military Stabilisation and Support Group operations
- Psychological Operations Group (15 PsyOps Group)
- 77th Brigade operations
- Dissemination of government-friendly content
- Countering foreign disinformation (e.g., Russian misinformation)
