### Key Units Inside the 77th Brigade

The 77th Brigade is organized into task forces and specialized groups rather than traditional fighting units.

* **5th Information Operations Task Force (5 IOTF):** The primary deployable element responsible for active operational planning, digital engagement, and executing targeted influence campaigns on the ground or online.
* **101 Information Operations Task Force (101 IOTF):** Primarily composed of Reservists who provide specialized, remote ("stand-off") support in areas like media analysis, web strategy, and digital forensics.
* **6th Military Intelligence Battalion (6 MI Bn):** Provides specialized intelligence support to analyze target audiences, track information networks, and assess local conditions.
* **Defence Cultural Specialist Unit (DCSU):** Provides deep cultural, linguistic, and demographic analysis of conflict zones so the military understands local populations.
* **Specialist Mentoring & Coaching Group:** Focuses on training foreign partner forces and providing advisory support to overseas militaries.
* **Honourable Artillery Company (HAC) elements:** A historic reserve regiment integrated into the brigade to provide Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR) support.
