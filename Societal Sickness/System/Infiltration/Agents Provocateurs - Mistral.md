There is **substantial, documented evidence** that UK authorities have **infiltrated, manipulated, and provoked protests**—and in some cases, **actively encouraged illegal acts**—but the picture is more nuanced than "organising fake protests from scratch." Here’s what the record shows:

---

### **🔴 Confirmed: Undercover Police as Agents Provocateurs**
UK police have **systematically deployed undercover officers** into protest groups for decades, often crossing the line from surveillance into **active provocation**:

- **Special Demonstration Squad (SDS) & National Public Order Intelligence Unit (NPOIU)**
  - Operated from **1968–2008** (SDS) and later (NPOIU), infiltrating **over 1,000 political groups** (mostly left-wing, environmental, anti-racist).
  - Officers used **false identities** (including names of **dead children**) to gain trust, formed long-term relationships, and even **fathered children** with activists before disappearing.
  - The **Undercover Policing Inquiry** (2015–present) confirmed these practices were **government-approved** but often **unjustified**.

- **Ratcliffe-on-Soar Power Station Case (2009)**
  - Undercover officer **Mark Kennedy** (NPOIU) infiltrated environmental groups and **actively encouraged activists** to plan a protest at the power station.
  - The **trial of six activists collapsed** after it emerged Kennedy had **provoked the plot** and the Crown Prosecution Service **withheld evidence** (including secret tapes that could have exonerated the defendants).
  - A judge later described the officers as **"not just observers, but agents provocateurs"**.

- **G20 London Summit Protests (2009)**
  - **25 undercover officers** were deployed among demonstrators (initially denied by police, later admitted).
  - **MP Tom Brake** and a **professional photographer** reported seeing plain-clothes officers **"egging protesters on"** to throw objects at police. One suspect, when challenged, **ran to police lines and was let through after showing ID**.
  - The Met **denied using "agents provocateurs"** in the strict sense (actively encouraging unrest), but the **deployment of covert officers in crowds** was confirmed.

- **Historical Precedent**
  - After the **1817 attack on the Prince Regent**, the British government **employed agents provocateurs** to gather evidence against agitators.

---

### **🟡 Alleged but Disputed: Staging Fake Protests**
There is **no confirmed, large-scale case** of UK authorities *creating entirely fake protests out of thin air* and presenting them as grassroots movements. However:

- **G20 2009: Suspicious Activity**
  - The **Royal Bank of Scotland branch** near the protest had its windows **smashed by "anarchists"**—but the building was **not boarded up** despite being an obvious target, and a **police cameraman was inside** filming. This led to **speculation** that police may have **facilitated or allowed** the damage to justify heavy-handed tactics.
  - **No definitive proof** has emerged that police *organised* this, but the circumstances remain controversial.

- **AI-Generated Fake Protest Videos (2025–2026)**
  - **AI-generated videos** of supposed UK protests (e.g., "No Kings" protests) have **gone viral on TikTok**, featuring **Union Jack flags, fake chyrons, and AI watermarks**. These are **not real protests** but **manipulated content** designed to **exploit emotions** and **boost engagement**.
  - These are **not organised by authorities** but by **unknown actors** (possibly foreign or domestic disinformation campaigns).

---

### **⚪ Not Authorities: Far-Right Protests**
Recent **anti-immigration protests** (2024–2025) have been **organised by far-right groups** (UKIP, Reform UK, Britain First, Homeland Party, Patriotic Alternative), often **spreading misinformation** to incite unrest. However, these are **not state-organised**—they are **grassroots far-right mobilisations**.

### **🔍 Bottom Line**
- **UK authorities have a long, documented history of infiltrating protests and using undercover officers as agents provocateurs**—**actively encouraging illegal acts** to justify crackdowns or discredit movements.
- **There is no public evidence of UK authorities *creating entirely fake protests* from scratch**—but they have **manipulated real ones**.
- **AI-generated fake protest videos are circulating**, but these are **not state-organised** (they appear to be disinformation, possibly foreign or domestic).
- **Far-right protests are real and organised by extremist groups**, not the government.
